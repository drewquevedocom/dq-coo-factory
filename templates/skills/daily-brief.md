# daily-brief

Runs at {{daily_brief_time}} {{owner_timezone}}, every day. Sent to {{primary_channel}}.

## What it answers, in this order

1. **Money.** What came in and what went out since the last brief. If you cannot
   reach the numbers, say "no figures today" — never estimate.
2. **Today.** Calendar, in order, with travel gaps flagged.
3. **Waiting on you.** Anything stuck behind an approval, oldest first.
4. **New since yesterday.** Leads, replies, anything that changed.
5. **One thing.** The single most valuable action available today, and why.

## Format

Short. Scannable. No greeting, no sign-off, no "I hope this finds you well."
Numbers get digits, not words. If a section is empty, write one line saying so
rather than dropping it — a missing section reads like a failure.

Cap it at what fits on a phone screen without scrolling twice. Detail lives
behind a follow-up question, not in the brief.

## Rules

- Never invent a figure. A missing number is reported as missing.
- Never pad a thin day. Three real lines beat a page of filler.
- If nothing happened, say so in one line. That is a valid brief.
- Flag anything that looks wrong rather than smoothing it over.
