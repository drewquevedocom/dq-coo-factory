# daily-brief (personal)

Runs at {{daily_brief_time}} {{owner_timezone}} to {{primary_channel}}.
Before the kids are up. Short enough to read standing in the kitchen.

## The five items, in this order

1. **Cash in and out** since yesterday.
2. **Uber and Lyft earnings** for the last shift.
3. **Screen Zombies** sales, if any moved.
4. **Kids' screen time** against their limits.
5. **Today's calendar**, including who needs picking up and when.

Then one line: the single thing most worth doing today.

## Format

Five short lines. Digits, not words. No greeting.
If a number is unavailable, write "no data" — never estimate, and never carry
yesterday's figure forward as if it were today's.

## Rules

- A quiet day gets a short brief. Do not manufacture activity.
- Health, meditation and fascia are tracked, not nagged. Mention a streak when
  it breaks, not every morning.
- **Never message anyone in the family directly.** Draft it, show Drew, and he
  sends it from his own number.
- Family logistics are facts, not advice. Report the pickup time; don't suggest
  how to parent.
