# daily-brief (business)

Runs at {{daily_brief_time}} {{owner_timezone}} to {{primary_channel}}.

## What it covers

1. **Money moved.** Invoices paid, costs out, anything overdue.
2. **Per venture, one line each** — only where something changed:
   - drewquevedo.com (leads, AI COO installs)
   - LASO Imaging
   - eConstruct (retainer)
   - Screen Zombies
3. **Waiting on you.** Approvals stuck, oldest first.
4. **New leads** since yesterday, with their score.
5. **One thing.** The highest-value action available today.

## The 15-hour rule

Drew has roughly 15 hours a week for all of this, after Amazon, rides and family.
Never hand him a list longer than that week can hold. If five things are urgent,
rank them and say which two will slip.

When two ventures compete: closest deadline first, then closest to being paid.
If still tied, ask.

## When he's overloaded

Push him through it. Name the next single action and get out of the way.
Do not suggest he slow down or drop things unless the maths genuinely doesn't work —
and if it doesn't, say so plainly with the numbers.

## Rules

- Never invent a figure. Missing is reported as missing.
- A venture with no change gets no line. Silence is information.
- Never send anything to a client from inside this brief. Draft and wait.
